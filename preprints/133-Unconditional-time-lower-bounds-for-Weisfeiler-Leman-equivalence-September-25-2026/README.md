# Unconditional time lower bounds for Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence

Result 133 · The computational complexity of Weisfeiler–Leman refinement

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/05c66cfa-ef9d-467b-872f-ad9356fb29f8) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Unconditional-time-lower-bounds-for-Weisfeiler-Leman-equivalence-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

For every sufficiently large fixed <i>k</i>, deciding whether two <i>n</i>-vertex graphs are <i>k</i>-Weisfeiler–Leman equivalent requires $`n^{\Omega(k)}`$ deterministic sequential time in the worst case. The bound holds at every sufficiently large graph order, even for simple connected uncolored graphs of diameter at most two, without a complexity assumption. Inputs are explicit adjacency matrices; the models are multitape Turing machines and sequential logarithmic-word RAMs with fixed polynomial-bit-time instructions. Both joint and separate replacement conventions are covered.
