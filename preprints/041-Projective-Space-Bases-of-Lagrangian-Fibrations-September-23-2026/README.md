# Projective-space bases of Lagrangian fibrations

Result 041 · Hyperkähler SYZ and projective-space bases

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/4143aee0-1f85-44af-bb74-9b005049645c) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Projective-Space-Bases-of-Lagrangian-Fibrations-September-23-2026/main.pdf)

We prove that the normal projective base of a projective Lagrangian fibration from a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is projective space. This resolves the projective-space base conjecture for such fibrations in every dimension and deformation type.
