# Uniform Stability of the Spherical Laughlin Gap

Result 269 · Uniform Laughlin gap and stability under bounded scalar disorder

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/967fae9e-5022-4009-954f-cf379045ee52) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Uniform-Stability-of-the-Spherical-Laughlin-Gap-October-5-2026/uniform-stability-spherical-laughlin-gap.pdf)

We prove that the fermionic Laughlin <i>V</i><sub>1</sub> Hamiltonian at filling 1/3 on expanding round spheres retains a unique ground state and a uniform spectral gap under sufficiently weak bounded real scalar one-body potentials projected to the lowest Landau level. With coefficient one for each pair projector and Laughlin flux $`q=3(N-1)`$, the gap and perturbation threshold are uniform for all sufficiently large particle numbers and all potential profiles of supremum norm at most one. The result uses the uniform unperturbed Fock-space gap and gives existential constants.
