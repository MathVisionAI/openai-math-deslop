# The Isoperimetric Conjecture for the Cubic Flat Three-Torus

Result 354 · The isoperimetric profile of the cubic three-torus

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d27fd2ec-90ce-46c6-8a5c-62eb2bf69d53) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Isoperimetric-Conjecture-for-the-Cubic-Flat-Three-Torus-September-24-2026/article.pdf)

We prove the isoperimetric conjecture for the cubic flat three-torus and classify all minimizers, including the equality cases at the transition volumes $`4\pi/81`$ and $`1/\pi`$. The minimizing regions are balls, circular tubes about shortest closed geodesics, coordinate slabs, and their complements.
