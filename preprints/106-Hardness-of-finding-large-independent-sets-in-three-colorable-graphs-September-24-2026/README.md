# Hardness of finding large independent sets in three-colorable graphs

Result 106 · Hardness of coloring three-colorable graphs

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/af6530c7-acc2-4e3e-9511-c1c670fa47db) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Hardness-of-finding-large-independent-sets-in-three-colorable-graphs-September-24-2026/Hardness-of-finding-large-independent-sets-in-three-colorable-graphs-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove that, for every fixed $`0\lt \delta\lt 1/3`$, it is NP-hard to distinguish three-colorable graphs from graphs in which every independent set has fewer than <i>δ</i> times the number of vertices. Consequently, for every fixed integer <i>c</i> ≥ 3, finding a proper <i>c</i>-coloring of a three-colorable graph is NP-hard.
