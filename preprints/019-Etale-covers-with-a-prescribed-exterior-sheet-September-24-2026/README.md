# Étale covers with a prescribed exterior sheet

Result 019 · The local <i>p</i>-adic section conjecture and global consequences

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f3046141-01bd-4337-a809-9ec2fc96191f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Etale-covers-with-a-prescribed-exterior-sheet-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

Let <i>X</i> be a smooth proper hyperbolic curve over an algebraic closure of a <i>p</i>-adic local field. Given finitely many disjoint small open disks, we construct one connected finite étale cover with a sheet isomorphic to their entire exterior and with degree divisible by <i>p</i> on every connected component above each disk.
