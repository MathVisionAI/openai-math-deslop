# The joint Dickman law for consecutive integers

Result 012 · Independent largest prime factors of consecutive integers

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/c18cff1a-aa8a-4fd5-a18b-a08b7b8431b8) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-joint-Dickman-law-for-consecutive-integers-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

Let $`P^+(n)`$ denote the largest prime factor of <i>n</i>. We prove that $`\log P^+(n)/\log n`$ and $`\log P^+(n+1)/\log n`$ are asymptotically independent in ordinary natural density, with Dickman marginals. This resolves the Erdős–Pomerance joint Dickman conjecture positively and implies that the ordering $`P^+(n)\lt P^+(n+1)`$ has natural density 1/2.
