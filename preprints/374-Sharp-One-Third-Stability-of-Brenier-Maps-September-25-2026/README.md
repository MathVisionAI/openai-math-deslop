# Sharp One-Third Stability of Brenier Maps

Result 374 · Sharp one-third stability of Brenier maps

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/fed0c71d-c1cf-4619-80ce-1476820ee007) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Sharp-One-Third-Stability-of-Brenier-Maps-September-25-2026/article.pdf)

For the uniform probability measure <i>ρ</i> on a compact convex body in ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup>, <i>d</i> ≥ 2, quadratic optimal transport maps are one-third Hölder continuous in $`L^2(\rho)`$ with respect to the target's 2-Wasserstein distance. The constant is uniform over all targets supported in a fixed compact set, and the exponent is optimal. A three-atom family on a fixed cube disproves Letrouit's conjectured uniform square-root estimate.
