# Cylinder amplitudes and logarithmic bridge-length windows on the honeycomb lattice

Result 237 · The three-quarter exponent for honeycomb self-avoiding walk

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/cb6c630e-1399-4005-9540-8d37467e7aff) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Cylinder-amplitudes-and-logarithmic-bridge-length-windows-on-the-honeycomb-lattice-September-26-2026/main.pdf)

We prove a logarithmic window of critical honeycomb bridge lengths: summed through height $`2h(\log h)^{1/64}`$, bridges of lengths between $`h^{4/3}(\log h)^{-1/8}`$ and $`2h^{4/3}(\log h)^{1/2}`$ have mass at least $`h^{3/4}(\log h)^{-C}`$.
