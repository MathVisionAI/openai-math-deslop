# Spectral scalar curvature and uniform Urysohn width

Result 336 · Spectral scalar curvature, Urysohn width, and macroscopic dimension

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f3efd1aa-2dee-49ab-8ef8-4ce1995a08f2) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Spectral-scalar-curvature-and-uniform-Urysohn-width-October-5-2026/main.pdf)

For every <i>n</i> ≥ 4, a complete connected smooth Riemannian <i>n</i>-manifold without boundary satisfying $`-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge1`$ as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most $`n-2`$ whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of <i>n</i>. The bound is measured in the original metric. This extends the uniform Urysohn width theorem from a pointwise scalar-curvature lower bound to a spectral lower bound.
