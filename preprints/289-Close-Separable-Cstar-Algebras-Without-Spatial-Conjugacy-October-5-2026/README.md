# Close Separable C*-Algebras Without Spatial Conjugacy

Result 289 · Strong Kadison–Kastler stability and its spatial boundaries

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/1908a26a-bf8e-41fa-aa40-d06b5a38c126) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Close-Separable-Cstar-Algebras-Without-Spatial-Conjugacy-October-5-2026/paper.pdf)

We disprove the separable <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebraic spatial form of the Kadison–Kastler conjecture. For every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0, we construct unital, norm-separable <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras on a common separable complex Hilbert space, with the same identity and Kadison–Kastler distance less than <i>ε</i>, that are not conjugate by any unitary. The two algebras have the same von Neumann closure.
