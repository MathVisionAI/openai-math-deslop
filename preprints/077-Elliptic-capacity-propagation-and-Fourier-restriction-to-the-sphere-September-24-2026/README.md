# Elliptic capacity propagation and Fourier restriction to the sphere

Result 077 · Fourier restriction for positively curved surfaces

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/e0572d67-00cf-4a56-b468-f696b5e18b8d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Elliptic-capacity-propagation-and-Fourier-restriction-to-the-sphere-September-24-2026/Elliptic-capacity-propagation-and-Fourier-restriction-to-the-sphere-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the bounded-data Fourier restriction conjecture for the sphere in three dimensions: the Fourier extension operator maps $`L^\infty(S^2)`$ boundedly into $`L^p(\mathbb R^3)`$ for every <i>p</i> &gt; 3. This is the full conjectured open range, and the threshold <i>p</i> = 3 is sharp.
