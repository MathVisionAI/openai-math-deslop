# The Dimension of the Two-Adic Hecke Algebra at Odd Level

Result 010 · Unrestricted pro-modularity at the prime two

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/bf9c2594-bbca-476d-bbba-b575e6d87be5) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Dimension-of-the-Two-Adic-Hecke-Algebra-at-Odd-Level-October-5-2026/two-adic-hecke.pdf)

For every odd positive integer <i>N</i>, every irreducible component of the full two-adic Hecke algebra of level $`\Gamma_1(N)`$ has Krull dimension four. This proves the <i>p</i> = 2 case of Emerton's dimension conjecture, including all residual components.
