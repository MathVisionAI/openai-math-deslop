# Maximal multipoint Seshadri constants in positive characteristic

Result 039 · Nagata’s conjecture and maximal Seshadri constants

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/47e4d70b-b044-4707-82ca-f7d3b93cbc32) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Maximal-multipoint-Seshadri-constants-in-positive-characteristic-October-5-2026/seshadri-positive-characteristic.pdf)

Let <i>L</i> be an ample line bundle on a smooth integral projective variety <i>X</i> over an algebraically closed field of positive characteristic, with $`\dim X=n\ge3`$. We prove that there is a threshold $`r_0=r_0(X,L)`$ such that for every integer $`r\ge r_0`$, the ordinary multipoint Seshadri constant at the geometric generic tuple of <i>r</i> points equals the volume bound $`(L^n/r)^{1/n}`$. The same conclusion holds in dimension two. This establishes the positive-characteristic form of the qualitative Nagata–Biran–Szemberg assertion at geometric generic tuples.
