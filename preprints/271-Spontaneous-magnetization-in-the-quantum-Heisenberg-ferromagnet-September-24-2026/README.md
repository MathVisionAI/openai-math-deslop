# Spontaneous magnetization in the quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet

Result 271 · Bloch's law, its lattice correction, and the spherical magnetization law

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f58200b5-d66f-4325-b70e-fe436729be81) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Spontaneous-magnetization-in-the-quantum-Heisenberg-ferromagnet-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

For every dimension <i>d</i> ≥ 3 and every spin $`S\in\{\frac12,1,\frac32,\ldots\}`$, we prove that the nearest-neighbor isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet has a translation-invariant, spontaneously magnetized equilibrium state at every sufficiently low positive temperature. The same state satisfies the KMS condition for the zero-field dynamics and has magnetization at least $`S/4`$. This resolves the low-temperature ordering problem in the spontaneous-magnetization formulation.
