# Abundance in numerical dimension one for terminal threefolds in positive characteristic

Result 035 · Log-canonical threefold abundance in numerical dimension one

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8653059b-91b4-4b7a-8cdb-92b8a95b3701) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Abundance-in-numerical-dimension-one-for-terminal-threefolds-in-positive-characteristic-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

Let <i>X</i> be a projective ℚ-factorial terminal threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic <i>p</i> &gt; 3. We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of abundance: if <i>K</i><sub><i>X</i></sub> is nef with $`\nu(K_X)=1`$, then <i>K</i><sub><i>X</i></sub> is semiample and $`\kappa(X,K_X)=1`$.
