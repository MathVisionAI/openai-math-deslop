# Weighted dilation graphs, smooth shifted primes and totient fibers

Result 011 · Prime-factor statistics of $`p-1`$

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/207c52d2-4df4-457e-b07c-c994220a0098) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Weighted-Dilation-Graphs-Smooth-Shifted-Primes-and-Totient-Fibers-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Erdős's conjecture on the largest fibers of Euler's totient function: for every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0, infinitely many positive integers <i>n</i> have more than $`n^{1-\varepsilon }`$ preimages. We also show that, for every fixed <i>δ</i> &gt; 0, there are at least $`x^{1-o(1)}`$ primes <i>p</i> in $`2x\lt p\le5x`$ whose predecessors have no prime factor exceeding <i>x</i><sup><i>δ</i></sup>.
