# A CH obstruction to a prescribed categoricity threshold

Result 240 · Shelah's eventual categoricity and the prescribed-threshold obstruction

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f2702eb6-dc45-43e8-a5ce-b5b367663c85) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-CH-Obstruction-to-a-Prescribed-Categoricity-Threshold-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

Assuming the continuum hypothesis, we construct an abstract elementary class with Löwenheim–Skolem number ℵ<sub>0</sub> that is categorical in every sufficiently large cardinal but has at least two nonisomorphic models of cardinality $`\beth_{\omega_2}`$. Thus categoricity does not transfer down to the proposed bound $`\beth_{(2^{\aleph_0})^+}`$, which equals $`\beth_{\omega_2}`$ under CH. Consequently, if ZFC is consistent, the prescribed-threshold form of Shelah's categoricity conjecture is not provable in ZFC.
