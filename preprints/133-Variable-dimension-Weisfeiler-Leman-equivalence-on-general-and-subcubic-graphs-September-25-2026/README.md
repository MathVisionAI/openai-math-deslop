# Variable-dimension Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence on general and subcubic graphs

Result 133 · The computational complexity of Weisfeiler–Leman refinement

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/1496cbce-175d-46e9-b03a-45021517929b) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Variable-dimension-Weisfeiler-Leman-equivalence-on-general-and-subcubic-graphs-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

Deciding joint-update <i>k</i>-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence is $`\mathsf{EXPTIME}`$-complete when the two graphs are given by explicit adjacency matrices and <i>k</i> ≥ 2 is encoded in binary. The result holds even for connected simple uncolored graphs of equal positive order and maximum degree at most three.
