# Open Homomorphisms of Global Solvably Closed Galois Groups

Result 031 · Uchida’s conjecture for open homomorphisms of Galois groups

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/870d3f7c-6a08-4163-a392-e65a0d6049b6) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Open-Homomorphisms-of-Global-Solvably-Closed-Galois-Groups-October-5-2026/open-homomorphisms-solvably-closed-galois-groups.pdf)

We prove Uchida's conjecture on open homomorphisms of Galois groups. Every continuous open homomorphism between Galois groups of solvably closed Galois extensions of number fields is induced by a unique equivariant field embedding in the opposite direction. No restriction on the kernel or additional compatibility hypothesis is required.
