# Compatibility entropy and the spectrum of a Thorp sweep

Result 238 · Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8273e8ac-40cf-450c-9755-dd6bd524fcec) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Compatibility-entropy-and-the-spectrum-of-a-Thorp-sweep-September-26-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the Thorp shuffle on $`n=2^d`$ cards mixes in $`\Theta(d)`$ physical shuffles. After a sufficiently large fixed number of coordinate sweeps, the total-variation distance of the full permutation from uniform tends to zero as $`d\to\infty`$.
