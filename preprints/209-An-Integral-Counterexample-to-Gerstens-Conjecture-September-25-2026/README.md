# An integral counterexample to Gersten's conjecture

Result 209 · Integral counterexamples to Gersten’s conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/32cfba66-c8a5-4ee6-a8b9-bf2267503933) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/An-Integral-Counterexample-to-Gerstens-Conjecture-September-25-2026/An-Integral-Counterexample-to-Gerstens-Conjecture-September-25-2026.pdf)

We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring <i>A</i> of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which $`K_5(A)\to K_5(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has a nonzero kernel. This disproves Gersten's conjecture in its unrestricted integral form.
