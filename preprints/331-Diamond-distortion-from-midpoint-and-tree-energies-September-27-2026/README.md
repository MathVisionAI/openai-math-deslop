# Distortion of countably branching diamonds from midpoint and tree energies

Result 331 · Reflexive midpoint convexity and diamond distortion

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8a3b9b69-921d-451b-bfff-85b32748049f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Diamond-distortion-from-midpoint-and-tree-energies-September-27-2026/manuscript.pdf)

We derive quantitative distortion bounds for countably branching diamond graphs from midpoint estimates and direct tree energies. In the dual of a finite-height segment forest, every distortion-<i>D</i> embedding of the depth-<i>k</i> diamond satisfies $`D^2\ge1+k/4`$. The same bound holds in the infinite-height coordinate predual. We also obtain power-type distortion bounds for path-cost and recursive tree norms.
