# A Fock-space inequality and the Laughlin spectral gap

Result 269 · Uniform Laughlin gap and stability under bounded scalar disorder

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/75ce39ba-cdf1-4e58-8ec0-5355ae0c1584) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-Fock-space-inequality-and-the-Laughlin-spectral-gap-September-24-2026/A-Fock-space-inequality-and-the-Laughlin-spectral-gap-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the spherical fermionic Laughlin spectral-gap conjecture for the full <i>V</i><sub>1</sub> interaction. With coefficient one for each pair projector, the gap above the Laughlin state at filling 1/3 on the round sphere is at least 1/25 for all sufficiently large systems. More generally, $`H_Q^2\ge\gamma H_Q`$ for some fixed $`\gamma\gt 1/25`$ on the entire lowest-Landau-level Fock space for all sufficiently large flux <i>Q</i>, independently of particle number.
