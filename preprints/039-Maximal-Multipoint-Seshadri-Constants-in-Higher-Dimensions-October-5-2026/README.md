# Maximal Multipoint Seshadri Constants in Higher Dimensions

Result 039 · Nagata’s conjecture and maximal Seshadri constants

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/98fa0e8a-68e4-405d-ad9a-900163175160) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Maximal-Multipoint-Seshadri-Constants-in-Higher-Dimensions-October-5-2026/main.pdf)

Let <i>L</i> be an ample line bundle on a smooth integral complex projective variety <i>X</i> of dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 3. We prove that there is a threshold $`r_0=r_0(X,L)`$ such that for every integer $`r\ge r_0`$, the ordinary multipoint Seshadri constant at <i>r</i> very general points equals the volume bound $`(L^n/r)^{1/n}`$. This establishes the qualitative Nagata–Biran–Szemberg assertion in these dimensions.
