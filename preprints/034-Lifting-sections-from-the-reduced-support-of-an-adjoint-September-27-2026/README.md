# Lifting sections from the reduced support of an adjoint

Result 034 · Log abundance for compact Kähler spaces under logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/ce7084ad-466f-4e78-a016-4601da9ebc07) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Lifting-sections-from-the-reduced-support-of-an-adjoint-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)

For a projective ℚ-factorial dlt pair with effective rational boundary over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, we prove that the adjoint has positive Iitaka dimension whenever a nonzero effective Cartier multiple is supported on the coefficient-one boundary and restricts to a semiample line bundle on its whole reduced support. This gives log abundance after nonvanishing in dimension at most four over ℂ.
