# Nuclear dimension and Jiang–Su stability without elementary subquotients

Result 291 · Cuntz comparison, nuclear dimension, and equivariant Jiang–Su stability

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/39fb9cb0-3ac0-4087-b683-06f95f638b58) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Nuclear-dimension-and-Jiang-Su-stability-without-elementary-subquotients-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

For separable nuclear <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras with no nonzero elementary ideal subquotients, finite nuclear dimension is equivalent to Jiang–Su stability. More generally, $`\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}(A_0\otimes\mathcal Z)\le1`$ for every separable nuclear <i>A</i><sub>0</sub>. This proves Robert and Tikuisis's Conjecture (C1) and the nuclear-dimension equivalence in the nonsimple Toms–Winter regularity question.
