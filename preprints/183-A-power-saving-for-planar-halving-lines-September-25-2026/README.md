# A power saving for planar halving lines

Result 183 · Power savings for planar halving lines and <i>k</i>-sets

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/ee3689e5-4ca7-46fb-a0a3-054cb9b05174) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-power-saving-for-planar-halving-lines-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

There are absolute constants <i>ε</i> &gt; 0 and <i>C</i> such that every sufficiently large even <i>n</i>-point set in the plane with no three collinear has at most $`Cn^{4/3-\varepsilon}`$ unordered halving pairs. This gives a power saving over the classical $`O(n^{4/3})`$ bound for planar halving lines. The proof is nonquantitative and does not supply explicit constants.
