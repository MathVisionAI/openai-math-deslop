# Bochner–Riesz multipliers in three dimensions

Result 078 · The three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d32811ee-fef4-4089-a260-90f2a6fd861e) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Bochner-Riesz-Multipliers-in-Three-Dimensions-September-24-2026/Bochner-Riesz-Multipliers-in-Three-Dimensions-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict-order formulation. The main result is boundedness of the Bochner–Riesz multipliers on $`L^3(\mathbb R^3)`$ for every positive order. Interpolation and duality then give the full conjectured strict range.
