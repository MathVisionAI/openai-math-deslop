# A rational obstruction to strong chromatic splitting at height three

Result 318 · Chromatic splitting: filtrations and counterexamples

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/199a6be4-b956-4c41-a9eb-b6a83b8d83b0) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-rational-obstruction-to-strong-chromatic-splitting-at-height-three-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

For every prime <i>p</i> ≥ 5, the canonical map $`L_0L_{K(3)}S\to L_0L_{K(2)}L_{K(3)}S`$ for the sphere spectrum <i>S</i> is nonzero on <i>π</i><sub>−3</sub>. Consequently, the height-three strong chromatic splitting formula is false in this range, even as an equivalence of underlying $`E(2)`$-local spectra without specified summand maps.
