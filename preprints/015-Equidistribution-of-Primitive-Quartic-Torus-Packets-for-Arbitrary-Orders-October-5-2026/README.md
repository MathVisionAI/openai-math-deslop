# Equidistribution of primitive quartic torus packets for arbitrary orders

Result 015 · Torus-packet equidistribution in prime, quartic, and sextic degrees

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/b27c4d02-ed13-4727-97e4-9e98a8644da4) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Equidistribution-of-Primitive-Quartic-Torus-Packets-for-Arbitrary-Orders-October-5-2026/quartic-torus-packets.pdf)

Let <i>K</i> range over totally real quartic fields with no proper intermediate field, and let $`\mathcal O`$ be any order in <i>K</i>. We prove that the packets of periodic diagonal orbits attached to invertible $`\mathcal O`$-ideal classes, weighted by orbit volume, equidistribute with no escape of mass as $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Disc}}\nolimits (\mathcal O)|`$ tends to infinity. The proof combines measure rigidity with a cubic-resolvent estimate that remains uniform at primes dividing the order index.
