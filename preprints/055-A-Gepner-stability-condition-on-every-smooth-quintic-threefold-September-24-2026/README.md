# A Gepner stability condition on every smooth quintic threefold

Result 055 · Gepner symmetry and large-volume stability on threefolds

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/00e8006e-4c0f-458b-bc4b-3af239fb31f1) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-Gepner-stability-condition-on-every-smooth-quintic-threefold-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Toda's normalized quintic Gepner conjecture. On every smooth complex quintic threefold, we construct a numerical Bridgeland stability condition for which tensoring by the hyperplane bundle, followed by the spherical twist at the structure sheaf, increases phase by 2/5.
