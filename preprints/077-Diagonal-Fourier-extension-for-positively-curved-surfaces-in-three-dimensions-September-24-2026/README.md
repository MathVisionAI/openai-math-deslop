# Diagonal Fourier extension for positively curved surfaces in three dimensions

Result 077 · Fourier restriction for positively curved surfaces

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/10f5c743-da6c-45aa-b23d-567d82e1aa4f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Diagonal-Fourier-extension-for-positively-curved-surfaces-in-three-dimensions-September-24-2026/Diagonal-Fourier-extension-for-positively-curved-surfaces-in-three-dimensions-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the diagonal Fourier extension conjecture for compact smooth positively curved surfaces $`\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^3`$, including surfaces with smooth boundary. For every $`3\lt p\lt \infty`$, the extension operator maps $`L^p(\Sigma)`$ boundedly into $`L^p(\mathbb R^3)`$. The compact paraboloid case also yields free Schrödinger local smoothing in two spatial dimensions for every $`3\lt p\lt \infty`$ and Sobolev order $`s\gt 2-6/p`$.
