# The Stable Hurewicz Image of the Sphere at Two

Result 316 · Curtis’s conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/adbde0e6-0db6-4fd0-a610-01b457728ced) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Stable-Hurewicz-Image-of-the-Sphere-at-Two-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Curtis's conjecture: in every positive degree, the mod-two stable Hurewicz image of the sphere is spanned by the images of <i>η</i>, <i>ν</i>, <i>σ</i> and the Kervaire-invariant-one classes that exist. Consequently, Eccles's conjecture holds for every sphere <i>S</i><sup><i>n</i></sup> with <i>n</i> &gt; 0.
