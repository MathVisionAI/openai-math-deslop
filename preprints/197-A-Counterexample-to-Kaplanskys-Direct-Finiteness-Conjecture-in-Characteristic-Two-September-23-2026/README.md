# A Counterexample to Kaplansky's Direct-Finiteness Conjecture in Characteristic Two

Result 197 · A torsion-free group algebra that is not directly finite

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/1591b4d5-ed18-46a0-84f3-65d30066e91c) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-Counterexample-to-Kaplanskys-Direct-Finiteness-Conjecture-in-Characteristic-Two-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We disprove Kaplansky's direct-finiteness conjecture by constructing a finite field <i>K</i> of characteristic two, a finitely presented group <i>G</i>, and finite sums $`a,b\in K[G]`$ with $`ab=1`$ but $`ba\ne1`$. The group <i>G</i> is nonsofic. The same elements define a cellular automaton on <i>K</i><sup><i>G</i></sup> that is injective but not surjective, disproving Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture.
