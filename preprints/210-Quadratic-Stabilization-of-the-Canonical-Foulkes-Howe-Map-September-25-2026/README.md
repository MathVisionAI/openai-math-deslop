# Quadratic stabilization of the canonical Foulkes--Howe map

Result 210 · Foulkes' conjecture for sixth powers and quadratic stabilization

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/e3849d2d-42be-40eb-bf66-8f2d63a407a1) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Quadratic-Stabilization-of-the-Canonical-Foulkes-Howe-Map-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

For every finite-dimensional complex vector space <i>V</i>, we prove that the canonical Foulkes–Howe map $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^a V)\longrightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^a(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b V)`$ is surjective whenever <i>a</i> ≥ 2 and $`b\ge a(a-1)`$. This gives a quadratic stabilization bound independent of $`\dim V`$.
