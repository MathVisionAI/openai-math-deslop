# Commuting Division-Coefficient Forms and the Artinian Eisenbud--Green--Harris Conjecture

Result 200 · Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/2b1d29fe-899f-4a5a-9779-ab087e08b9d7) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Commuting-Division-Coefficient-Forms-and-the-Artinian-Eisenbud-Green-Harris-Conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the Eisenbud–Green–Harris conjecture over every characteristic-zero field for homogeneous regular sequences of any positive length, with degrees at least two. Thus every homogeneous ideal containing such a sequence has the Hilbert function of a monomial ideal containing the corresponding pure powers.
