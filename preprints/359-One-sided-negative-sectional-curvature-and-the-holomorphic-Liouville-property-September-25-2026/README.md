# One-sided negative sectional curvature and the holomorphic Liouville property

Result 359 · Negative Kähler curvature without bounded holomorphic coordinates

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d7cefe7c-e033-4418-9f2b-a4f10d703f7f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/One-sided-negative-sectional-curvature-and-the-holomorphic-Liouville-property-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

We construct, in some sufficiently large fixed finite complex dimension <i>m</i>, a domain in ℂ<sup><i>m</i></sup> diffeomorphic to $`\mathbb R^{2m}`$ that admits a complete Kähler metric with real sectional curvature at most −1 and has only constant bounded holomorphic functions. Its sectional curvatures are unbounded below. The construction gives a negative answer to the one-sided bounded-holomorphic-function question.
