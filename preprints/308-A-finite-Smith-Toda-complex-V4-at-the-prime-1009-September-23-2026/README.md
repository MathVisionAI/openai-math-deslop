# A finite Smith–Toda complex V(4) at the prime 1009

Result 308 · Finite Smith–Toda complexes at every height

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/09b08bfb-d657-4cbf-b0d6-66130660ac7e) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-finite-Smith-Toda-complex-V4-at-the-prime-1009-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We construct a Smith–Toda complex $`V(4)`$ at the prime 1009. It is an ordinary finite 1009-local spectrum whose Brown–Peterson homology is $`BP_*/(1009,v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4)`$, with its canonical comodule structure and generator in degree zero.
