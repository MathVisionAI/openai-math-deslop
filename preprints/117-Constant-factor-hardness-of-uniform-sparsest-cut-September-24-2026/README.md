# Constant-factor hardness of uniform sparsest cut

Result 117 · Uniform sparsest cut: hardness and semidefinite gaps

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/986c4124-621d-4868-b9a2-1e3e857f66b7) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Constant-factor-hardness-of-uniform-sparsest-cut-September-24-2026/Constant-factor-hardness-of-uniform-sparsest-cut-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove that, for every fixed <i>C</i> &gt; 1, approximating Uniform Sparsest Cut within factor <i>C</i> is NP-hard. The output graphs have nonnegative rational capacities and unit demand between every pair of distinct vertices.
