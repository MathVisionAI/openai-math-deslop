# The maximum number of mutually unbiased bases in dimension six

Result 266 · Exactly three mutually unbiased bases in dimension six

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/278a14d4-786f-47dd-b4ee-c10122d16fca) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-maximum-number-of-mutually-unbiased-bases-in-dimension-six-September-24-2026/The-maximum-number-of-mutually-unbiased-bases-in-dimension-six-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove that the maximum number of mutually unbiased orthonormal bases in ℂ<sup>6</sup> is three, resolving Zauner's dimension-six MUB conjecture. The upper bound is computer-assisted: under the stated binary64 arithmetic and compiler conditions, a complete execution of the documented verification pipeline excludes four arbitrary complex bases.
