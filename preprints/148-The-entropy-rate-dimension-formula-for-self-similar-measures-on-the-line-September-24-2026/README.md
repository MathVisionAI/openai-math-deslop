# The entropy-rate dimension formula for self-similar measures on the line

Result 148 · The entropy-rate dimension formula for self-similar measures

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/b2f06b09-3a8a-42ef-8dd9-1d116756a2b2) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-entropy-rate-dimension-formula-for-self-similar-measures-on-the-line-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

We prove that the Hausdorff dimension of every finite real self-similar measure equals the minimum of one and its random-walk entropy rate divided by its Lyapunov exponent. Exact overlaps are allowed, and the contraction ratios may be unequal and negative. This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture.
