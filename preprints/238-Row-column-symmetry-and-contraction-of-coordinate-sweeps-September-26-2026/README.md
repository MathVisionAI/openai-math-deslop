# Row&#8211;column symmetry and contraction of coordinate sweeps

Result 238 · Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f23027a4-423b-4772-ad12-a64195b65943) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Row-column-symmetry-and-contraction-of-coordinate-sweeps-September-26-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the Thorp shuffle on $`N=2^d`$ labeled cards has full-permutation total-variation mixing time $`\Theta(\log N)`$ in physical shuffles. An absolute number of coordinate sweeps suffices for the upper bound.
