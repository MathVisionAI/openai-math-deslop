# Uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros

Result 003 · The quasi-Riemann hypothesis

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/4761cb77-c856-47ba-a46d-f36d043d6d71) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Uniform-exclusion-of-Landau-Siegel-zeros-October-1-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros. There is an absolute constant <i>c</i> &gt; 0 such that every real zero $`\beta\in(0,1)`$ of every primitive nonprincipal real Dirichlet <i>L</i>-function of conductor <i>q</i> ≥ 3 satisfies $`(1-\beta)\log q\ge c`$.
