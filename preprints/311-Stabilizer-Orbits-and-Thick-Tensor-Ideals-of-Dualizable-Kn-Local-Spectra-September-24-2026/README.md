# Stabilizer orbits and thick tensor ideals of dualizable K(n)-local spectra

Result 311 · The Hovey–Strickland and Chai conjectures

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/c4dd059d-9ddf-4503-90b0-0ec431f4db3d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Stabilizer-Orbits-and-Thick-Tensor-Ideals-of-Dualizable-Kn-Local-Spectra-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We classify the prime and radical ideals of the Lubin–Tate deformation ring invariant under an open Morava stabilizer subgroup, at every prime and positive height. This proves Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture in the standard finite-residue-field formulation. It also proves the Hovey–Strickland conjecture: the dualizable $`K(n)`$-local category has exactly $`n+2`$ thick tensor ideals, and its Balmer spectrum is a chain of $`n+1`$ points.
