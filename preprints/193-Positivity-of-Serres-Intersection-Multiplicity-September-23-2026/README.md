# Positivity of Serre's Intersection Multiplicity

Result 193 · Serre’s intersection-multiplicity conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/76a165d9-9498-4e89-ac65-b4fe32d5e0aa) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Positivity-of-Serres-Intersection-Multiplicity-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Serre's positivity conjecture, including ramified mixed characteristic. If two nonzero finitely generated modules over a regular local ring have tensor product of finite length and complementary dimensions, then their intersection multiplicity is strictly positive.
