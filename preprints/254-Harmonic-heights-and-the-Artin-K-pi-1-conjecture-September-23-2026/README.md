# Harmonic heights and the Artin K(pi,1) conjecture

Result 254 · Classifying spaces and geometric obstructions for Artin groups

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/81df244c-5a36-4642-9981-8871b6518ad6) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Harmonic-heights-and-the-Artin-K-pi-1-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the standard Salvetti complex of every Artin group with finitely many standard generators is aspherical. This resolves the Artin $`K(\pi,1)`$ conjecture in finite rank.
