# Virasoro Constraints for Projective Complete Intersections

Result 065 · Virasoro constraints for complete intersections and projective-bundle towers

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/1cfc17ca-e444-406d-bdae-751d9962a0f6) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Virasoro-Constraints-for-Projective-Complete-Intersections-September-24-2026/article.pdf)

We prove the Virasoro conjecture for the ordinary descendant Gromov–Witten theory of smooth complete intersections in projective space, in every genus and curve class and with arbitrary cohomology insertions. This includes primitive and odd cohomology classes, with no semisimplicity assumption.
