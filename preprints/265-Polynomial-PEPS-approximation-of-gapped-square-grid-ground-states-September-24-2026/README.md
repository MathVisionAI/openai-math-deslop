# Polynomial PEPS approximation of gapped square-grid ground states

Result 265 · Area laws and tensor networks for two-dimensional gapped systems

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d074779b-df7a-409d-aa37-43cb8bdbf7d8) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Polynomial-PEPS-approximation-of-gapped-square-grid-ground-states-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the unique ground state of a uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian on an $`L\times L`$ square lattice admits a projected entangled-pair state approximation with bond dimension polynomial in <i>L</i> and global vector error at most <i>L</i><sup>−1</sup> after normalization. Only the gap of the full Hamiltonian is assumed. The result is an existence theorem, with constants uniform over Hamiltonians of fixed local dimension, interaction strength, and gap.
