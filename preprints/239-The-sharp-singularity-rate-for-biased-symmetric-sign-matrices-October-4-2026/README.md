# The sharp singularity rate for biased symmetric sign matrices

Result 239 · Sharp singularity rates for symmetric random sign matrices

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f0d6383b-ec2d-4082-8ff7-dab4eed36b23) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-sharp-singularity-rate-for-biased-symmetric-sign-matrices-October-4-2026/biased-symmetric-sign-singularity.pdf)

Let <i>A</i><sub><i>n</i></sub> be a symmetric random matrix whose entries on and above the diagonal are independent signs, equal to 1 with fixed probability $`p\in(0,1)\setminus\{1/2\}`$. We prove that $`\mathbb P(\det A_n=0)=(p^2+(1-p)^2+o(1))^n`$. The rate is attained by the event that two rows agree.
