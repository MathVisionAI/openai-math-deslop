# Topological triviality of mu-constant families of surface singularities

Result 064 · Topological triviality of <i>μ</i>-constant surface singularities

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d9d0c095-932e-4cef-b8bc-c7697e32ef41) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Topological-triviality-of-mu-constant-families-of-surface-singularities-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

We prove that every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in ℂ<sup>3</sup> with constant Milnor number is topologically right-trivial. This gives a positive answer to the surface case of the <i>μ</i>-constant problem. The trivialization fixes the parameter and the origin section, and preserves the defining functions.
