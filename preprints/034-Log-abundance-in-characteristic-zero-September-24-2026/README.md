# Log abundance in characteristic zero

Result 034 · Log abundance for compact Kähler spaces under logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/cc7438b0-46cc-4032-ac38-d8ab25366426) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Log-abundance-in-characteristic-zero-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the rational-boundary log abundance conjecture in every dimension over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero: every nef ℚ-Cartier log canonical divisor on a projective log canonical pair with effective rational boundary is semiample. Over ℂ, the proof also establishes canonical nonvanishing for smooth projective varieties in every dimension.
