# Removable Boundaries and Rigidity of Circle Domains

Result 071 · Koebe’s circle-domain conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/6476a32a-09f3-4b0b-b0b0-0fb5bbdb73af) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Removable-Boundaries-and-Rigidity-of-Circle-Domains-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that a circle domain with conformally removable boundary is conformally rigid, with no restriction on the number of complementary components. This establishes the removability-to-rigidity direction of the He–Schramm Conjecture.
