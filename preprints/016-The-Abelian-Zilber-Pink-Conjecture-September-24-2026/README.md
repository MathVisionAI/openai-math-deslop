# The abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture

Result 016 · Zilber–Pink in abelian varieties and the Siegel threefold

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/60a5a200-3a9b-4a59-a7e8-99a2a7eb5afd) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Abelian-Zilber-Pink-Conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture over $`\overline{\mathbb Q}`$. Every irreducible subvariety of an abelian variety has only finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties, where atypicality is measured inside its smallest containing torsion coset.
