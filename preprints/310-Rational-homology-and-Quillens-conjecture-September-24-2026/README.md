# Rational homology and Quillen's conjecture

Result 310 · Quillen's conjecture in rational homology

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/b0f3c269-6518-4dc5-bc4b-d77994e9fa33) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Rational-homology-and-Quillens-conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Quillen's conjecture for all finite groups and all primes. More precisely, if a finite group <i>G</i> has trivial largest normal <i>p</i>-subgroup $`O_p(G)`$, then the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian <i>p</i>-subgroups of <i>G</i> has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology. This establishes the stronger rational-homology form of the conjecture.
