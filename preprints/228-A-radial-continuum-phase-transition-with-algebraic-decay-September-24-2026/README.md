# A radial continuum phase transition with algebraic decay

Result 228 · Continuum phase transitions for radial pair potentials

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/a7d87f14-6052-4c54-8613-e66c4eb29ce1) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-radial-continuum-phase-transition-with-algebraic-decay-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We construct a bounded, continuous, stable radial pair potential in three dimensions with $`|\phi(r)|\le Cr^{-3-1/32}`$ for <i>r</i> ≥ 1. Throughout an open interval of positive densities, its canonical thermodynamic free energy is finite at every positive inverse temperature and has a strict downward derivative jump at one common finite positive inverse temperature.
