# Metric Markov Cotype Two of <i>ℓ</i><sub>1</sub> 

Result 332 · Metric Markov cotype of <i>ℓ</i><sub>1</sub> and Hilbert-space Lipschitz extension

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/259e936c-fd2d-4985-980c-9c0743c7abf3) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Metric-Markov-Cotype-Two-of-l1-October-5-2026/l1-markov-cotype.pdf)

We prove that the real Banach space <i>ℓ</i><sub>1</sub> has metric Markov cotype two, answering a question of Mendel and Naor. As a consequence, every Lipschitz map from an arbitrary subset of a real Hilbert space into <i>ℓ</i><sub>1</sub> extends to the whole Hilbert space with a universal multiplicative loss in its Lipschitz constant, resolving Ball's extension problem for this target.
