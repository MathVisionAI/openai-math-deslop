# From partial permutation information to Fourier bounds

Result 238 · Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8eb7b875-0288-4135-bcba-c07e7f996572) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/From-partial-permutation-information-to-Fourier-bounds-September-26-2026/main.pdf)

We show how information about partial permutations controls full permutation laws. Let <i>n</i> tend to infinity through multiples of eight. If the images of a uniformly chosen $`7n/8`$ labels approach the uniform injection law in average total variation, and the sign mean tends to zero, then the product of two independent permutations with the given law converges to uniform on <i>S</i><sub><i>n</i></sub>.
