# Projective Hodge lines and ordinary Iitaka subadditivity

Result 033 · Iitaka subadditivity, variation, and logarithmic additivity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/faa2f9d7-b82c-40bf-8d9f-a5ec8909493c) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Projective-Hodge-lines-and-ordinary-Iitaka-subadditivity-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the ordinary Iitaka subadditivity conjecture for surjective projective morphisms with connected fibers between smooth connected projective varieties over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero. If <i>F</i> is the geometric generic fiber of $`f:X\to Z`$, then $`\kappa(X)\geq\kappa(F)+\kappa(Z)`$.
