# Scalar Potentials and Slow Clocks for Forced Fluid Computation

Result 376 · Universal computation in forced Navier–Stokes flows

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/0a4496e7-f15b-4454-b57d-96f25519566d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Scalar-Potentials-and-Slow-Clocks-for-Forced-Fluid-Computation-September-27-2026/manuscript.pdf)

We construct smooth forces of fixed compact spatial support for three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes flow from rest whose particle at the origin detects halting by entering a fixed half-space. Every mixed derivative of the force and velocity decays at rate $`O((1+t)^{-1-j})`$ for time order <i>j</i>. Three scalar potentials lift a coded rectangle, perform its instruction, and lower its image; an unbounded logarithmic clock supplies the decay. We also realize area-changing prefix instructions on an invariant torus plane, and construct a planar Hamiltonian processor admitting periodic forcing, stationary forcing after startup, and a velocity-field detector through a companion diffusion theorem.
