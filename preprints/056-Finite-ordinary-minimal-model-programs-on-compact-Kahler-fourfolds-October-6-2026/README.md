# Finite ordinary minimal model programs on compact Kähler fourfolds

Result 056 · Termination of projective and Kähler fourfold minimal model programs

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/99519861-6fdb-4f9b-97fa-1fb279d08757) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Finite-ordinary-minimal-model-programs-on-compact-Kahler-fourfolds-October-6-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that every maximal ordinary negative-ray program starting from a compact Kähler klt fourfold pair with effective rational boundary in the global Weil-divisor ℚ-factorial category terminates. It ends at a nef model when the adjoint is pseudo-effective and at a projective Mori fibre space otherwise.
