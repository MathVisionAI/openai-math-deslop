# Bi-Lipschitz Coordinates at Regular Points of Noncollapsed RCD Spaces

Result 357 · Bi-Lipschitz coordinates at every regular RCD point

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/34b16cfc-0f5f-44e4-87d0-d3334d6cbf15) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Bi-Lipschitz-Coordinates-at-Regular-Points-of-Noncollapsed-RCD-Spaces-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

We resolve the regular-point bi-Lipschitz conjecture in the noncollapsed setting. For every integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2 and every real <i>K</i>, every regular point of a noncollapsed $`\mathrm{RCD}(K,n)`$ space has an open neighborhood bi-Lipschitz homeomorphic to an open subset of ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup>. The bi-Lipschitz constant depends only on <i>n</i>, and the neighborhood uses the restricted ambient distance.
