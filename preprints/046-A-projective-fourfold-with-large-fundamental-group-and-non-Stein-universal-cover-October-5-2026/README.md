# A projective fourfold with large fundamental group and non-Stein universal cover

Result 046 · Shafarevich counterexamples in dimension two and with large fundamental group

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/0736579e-f9bb-45d9-98f2-aa1064e0f93d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-projective-fourfold-with-large-fundamental-group-and-non-Stein-universal-cover-October-5-2026/large-fundamental-group-non-stein.pdf)

We construct a smooth projective complex fourfold with large fundamental group whose universal cover is not Stein. The universal cover contains no positive-dimensional compact complex-analytic subvariety, so this disproves Shafarevich's holomorphic-convexity conjecture even under the large-fundamental-group hypothesis.
