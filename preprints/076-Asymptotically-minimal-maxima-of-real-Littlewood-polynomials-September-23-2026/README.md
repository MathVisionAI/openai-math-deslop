# Asymptotically minimal maxima of real Littlewood polynomials

Result 076 · Real ultraflat Littlewood polynomials and unbounded binary merit factors

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/e8f56712-5957-431f-a3ad-9aa30f0d9c02) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Asymptotically-minimal-maxima-of-real-Littlewood-polynomials-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the minimum possible maximum modulus on the unit circle of a polynomial with <i>N</i> consecutive real coefficients in $`\{-1,1\}`$ is $`(1+o(1))\sqrt N`$, as <i>N</i> tends to infinity through all integers. This disproves the real-sign analogue of Erdős's fixed relative-gap conjecture. As a consequence, the largest binary merit factor at length <i>N</i> tends to infinity through all integer lengths, disproving Turyn's conjecture.
