For every <i>η</i> &gt; 0 and every sufficiently large integer <i>N</i>, there is a polynomial with <i>N</i> consecutive coefficients in $`\{-1,1\}`$ whose modulus lies between $`\sqrt N/16`$ and $`(1+\eta)\sqrt N`$ everywhere on the unit circle.
