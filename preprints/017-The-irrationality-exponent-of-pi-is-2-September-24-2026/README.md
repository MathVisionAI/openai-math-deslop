# The irrationality exponent of pi is 2

Result 017 · The irrationality exponent of <i>π</i> is 2

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/14fc738c-1141-4bb0-b00f-f29ab9bc7c28) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-irrationality-exponent-of-pi-is-2-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the conjecture that the irrationality exponent of <i>π</i> is 2. As a consequence, the classical Flint–Hills series $`\sum_{n\ge1}1/(n^3\sin^2 n)`$ converges, with angles in radians.
