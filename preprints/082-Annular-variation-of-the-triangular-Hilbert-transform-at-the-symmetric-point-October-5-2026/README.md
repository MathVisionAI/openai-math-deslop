# Annular variation of the triangular Hilbert transform at the symmetric point

Result 082 · Annular variation and dyadic absolute bounds for the triangular Hilbert transform

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/35e97473-6c16-4323-8ab0-6ea4bece2a85) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Annular-variation-of-the-triangular-Hilbert-transform-at-the-symmetric-point-October-5-2026/annular-variation.pdf)

We prove the annular <i>r</i>-variation estimate for the triangular Hilbert transform from complex $`L^3\times L^3`$ to <i>L</i><sup>3/2</sup> for every <i>r</i> &gt; 2. The partitions may depend on the output point and range over all positive scales. The estimate yields the two-endpoint maximal bound and joint almost-everywhere and norm principal values, and resolves the symmetric scalar triangular Hilbert transform problem.
