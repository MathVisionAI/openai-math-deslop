# Talagrand’s discrete-convexity conjecture

Result 175 · Talagrand’s expectation thresholds, discrete convexity, and graph decompositions

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/750ab5af-95aa-410d-9a42-21ff8ee6da7b) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Talagrands-discrete-convexity-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove Talagrand's discrete-convexity conjecture. There is a universal integer <i>k</i> such that, whenever an arbitrary family has Bernoulli product measure at least $`1-1/k`$, the sets not contained in a union of <i>k</i> members admit a cover of total cost at most 1/2 at the same density.
