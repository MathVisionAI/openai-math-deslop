# Minimal models and Mori fibre spaces for generalized log canonical Q-pairs

Result 036 · Numerical semiampleness and generalized minimal models

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/7e3e2d52-2437-492a-842d-74d45cb85125) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Minimal-models-and-Mori-fibre-spaces-for-generalized-log-canonical-Q-pairs-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We resolve the existence form of the minimal-model conjecture for projective generalized log canonical ℚ-pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero. Such a pair admits a minimal model when its adjoint divisor is pseudo-effective, and a Mori fibre space otherwise, while keeping the nef ℚ-Cartier b-divisor fixed. Taking the nef part to be zero gives the corresponding result for ordinary log canonical ℚ-pairs.
