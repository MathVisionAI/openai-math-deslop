# Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss

Result 314 · Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/9fc24e68-19bd-4603-884c-1b849ed414aa) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026.pdf)

For a finite <i>p</i>-group <i>G</i> and a subgroup <i>H</i>, we prove that the optimal chromatic fixed-point loss equals the shortest length of a subnormal chain from <i>H</i> to <i>G</i> with cyclic quotients. The equality holds at every prime and every nonnegative height, resolving positively the equality proposed by Kuhn and Lloyd.
