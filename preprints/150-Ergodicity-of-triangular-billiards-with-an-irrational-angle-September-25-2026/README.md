# Ergodicity of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

Result 150 · Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/0a953302-7eec-42bf-b6f3-f4f5da805a94) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Ergodicity-of-triangular-billiards-with-an-irrational-angle-September-25-2026/Ergodicity-of-triangular-billiards-with-an-irrational-angle-September-25-2026.pdf)

We prove that the unit-speed billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to <i>π</i> is ergodic for normalized area times uniform angular measure. No genericity or Diophantine condition is required. The flow is considered outside the null set of trajectories that hit a vertex.
