# Brownian continuum random tree limits of finite Fortuin–Kasteleyn maps above four

Result 211 · The geometric phase diagram, diffusion, and spectra of random planar maps

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8f1912d7-7fb2-432a-9642-5536a580ca09) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Brownian-continuum-random-tree-limits-of-finite-Fortuin-Kasteleyn-maps-above-four-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

We prove the finite-volume continuum-random-tree prediction for critical Fortuin–Kasteleyn planar maps at every fixed <i>q</i> &gt; 4. After rescaling graph distances by a constant times <i>n</i><sup>−1/2</sup>, an <i>n</i>-edge map with normalized degree measure converges to the Brownian continuum random tree in the Gromov–Hausdorff–Prokhorov topology. The convergence holds through all positive integer sizes.
