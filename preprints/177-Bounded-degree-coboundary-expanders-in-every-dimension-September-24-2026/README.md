# Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension

Result 177 · Bounded-degree coboundary expanders

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/6ab3ce76-dd19-42d9-8646-852b63d94279) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Bounded-degree-coboundary-expanders-in-every-dimension-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

For every integer <i>d</i> ≥ 3, we construct arbitrarily large finite <i>d</i>-dimensional simplicial complexes with uniformly bounded vertex degrees and uniform 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expansion in every degree below <i>d</i>. Together with the known graph and two-dimensional cases, this establishes the existence of bounded-degree 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expanders in every positive dimension.
