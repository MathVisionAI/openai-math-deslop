# Exact orders and invariant anticanonical linear systems

Result 068 · Anticanonical nonvanishing in every dimension

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/b7dc61b5-c63f-41c6-9dc4-1ca2249dd642) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Exact-orders-and-invariant-anticanonical-linear-systems-September-26-2026/main.pdf)

On a smooth connected projective complex variety with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle, we prove that asymptotically zero valuation on the curvature-null face is attained by an effective rational anticanonical divisor. The fixed auxiliary line bundle is arbitrary.
