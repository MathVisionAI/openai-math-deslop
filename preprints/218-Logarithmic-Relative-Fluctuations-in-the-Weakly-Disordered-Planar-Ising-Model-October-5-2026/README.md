# Logarithmic Relative Fluctuations in the Weakly Disordered Planar Ising Model

Result 218 · Conformal universality for weakly interacting and random-bond Ising models

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/3dc68c7f-3a30-4fef-82ac-3b351cf8ccd9) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Logarithmic-Relative-Fluctuations-in-the-Weakly-Disordered-Planar-Ising-Model-October-5-2026/critical-relative-second-moment-weak-random-bond-ising.pdf)

Assuming the stated deterministic critical-reference estimates, we prove that the relative second moment of the quenched critical spin correlation in the square-lattice Ising model with independent fair bonds $`1\pm\varepsilon`$ grows as $`(\log r)^{1/4+o(1)}`$ for each sufficiently small fixed <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. The correlation is evaluated at the physical critical temperature, with the thermodynamic limit taken before the disorder moments and the large-distance limit.
