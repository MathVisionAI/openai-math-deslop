# Critical mixing in the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model

Result 227 · Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition

At the critical inverse temperature <i>β</i> = 1, we determine the worst-start total-variation mixing exponent for single-site heat-bath dynamics in the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model. The mixing time is $`n^{2/3+o(1)}`$ when every site has rate one, or $`n^{5/3+o(1)}`$ attempted uniform-site updates, in probability over the disorder.
