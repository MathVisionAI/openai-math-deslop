# Minimal models in numerical dimension one

Result 036 · Numerical semiampleness and generalized minimal models

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/3325f20a-1644-4978-b0e8-175fdc36ba82) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Minimal-models-in-numerical-dimension-one-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We resolve the numerical-dimension-one case of the minimal-model conjecture for smooth connected complex projective varieties of dimension at least three. If <i>K</i><sub><i>X</i></sub> is pseudo-effective and $`\kappa_\sigma(X,K_X)=1`$, with <i>κ</i><sub><i>σ</i></sub> defined by section growth with a fixed ample twist, then <i>X</i> admits a projective ℚ-factorial terminal minimal model.
