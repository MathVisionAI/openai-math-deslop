# A directional zero–one law under strict ellipticity

Result 220 · Directional zero–one laws beyond iid environments and iid ballisticity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/96652350-8122-43ff-8865-bce230e1c08d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-directional-zero-one-law-under-strict-ellipticity-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the directional zero–one conjecture for nearest-neighbor random walks in independent and identically distributed strictly elliptic environments on ℤ<sup><i>d</i></sup>, <i>d</i> ≥ 3: the probability of escape in each fixed nonzero real direction is zero or one. Only strict positivity of the transition probabilities is required; no uniform lower bound or moment assumption is imposed.
