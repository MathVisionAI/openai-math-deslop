# Rigidity of Smooth Billiards with a Continuous Caustic Collar

Result 147 · The near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/cda2fc18-60ce-4e05-91d2-f298cd2a12ea) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Rigidity-of-Smooth-Billiards-with-a-Continuous-Caustic-Collar-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that a smooth strictly convex planar billiard with positive curvature is an ellipse whenever a full neighborhood of its boundary is continuously foliated by smooth closed convex caustics. No differentiability across the leaves is assumed. This resolves the near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture in the stated smooth class.
