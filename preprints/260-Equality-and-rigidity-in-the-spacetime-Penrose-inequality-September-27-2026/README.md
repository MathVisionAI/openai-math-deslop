# Equality and rigidity in the spacetime Penrose inequality

Result 260 · Spacetime Penrose inequalities: enclosing area, charge, rotation, and anti-de Sitter extensions

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/c223999f-358e-4cf9-812e-1158000f1f43) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Equality-and-rigidity-in-the-spacetime-Penrose-inequality-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)

Equality in the spacetime Penrose inequality identifies the original initial data as a spacelike slice of a Schwarzschild–Tangherlini exterior, smoothly attached to its horizon, under the stated outermostness and decay hypotheses. We prove this in every spatial dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 3 for a strong-decay exterior class and for weaker decay in dimensions three and four. In dimension three the finite-component theorem forces a single spherical horizon and gives qualitative strictness for disconnected boundaries.
