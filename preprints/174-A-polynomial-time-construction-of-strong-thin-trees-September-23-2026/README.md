# A polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees

Result 174 · Deterministic construction of strong thin spanning trees

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/04cca68b-92d6-4bfb-ab9e-128d7d18a760) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-polynomial-time-construction-of-strong-thin-trees-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We give a deterministic polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees. Given a finite <i>k</i>-edge-connected loopless multigraph on at least one vertex, the algorithm constructs a spanning tree meeting every cut in at most a $`C/k`$ fraction of its edges, for a universal constant <i>C</i>. The running time is polynomial in the binary input length, including when parallel-edge multiplicities are encoded in binary.
