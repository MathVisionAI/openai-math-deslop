# Generalized ionization energies for full Coulomb atoms

Result 263 · The ionization and generalized ionization conjectures

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/2f22e4fc-a8a0-4e86-a9fa-887e60e89955) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Generalized-ionization-energies-for-full-Coulomb-atoms-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the energy part of the generalized ionization conjecture for full nonrelativistic Coulomb atoms with two electron spin states. The energy needed to remove <i>m</i> electrons is asymptotic to $`a_{\mathrm{TF}}m^{7/3}`$ whenever $`m\to\infty`$ and $`Z/m\to\infty`$, with the Thomas–Fermi constant in atomic units. We also obtain both conjectured iterated limits.
