# Independent products in real L1: asymptotic midpoint convexity without AUC renormings

Result 331 · Reflexive midpoint convexity and diamond distortion

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/5e5d018c-6dc8-46a2-a716-0fd1e105f154) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Independent-products-in-real-L1-asymptotic-midpoint-convexity-without-AUC-renormings-September-27-2026/manuscript.pdf)

For a countably branching tree, the closed real <i>L</i><sup>1</sup> spans of products of independent exponential or Gaussian-square multipliers along its paths have positive averaged asymptotic midpoint moduli and admit no equivalent asymptotically uniformly convex norm. The renorming obstruction holds for every positive nonconstant mean-one multiplier with finite second moment.
