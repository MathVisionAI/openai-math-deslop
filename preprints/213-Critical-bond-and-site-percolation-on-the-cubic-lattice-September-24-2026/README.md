# Critical bond and site percolation on the cubic lattice

Result 213 · Critical percolation on every quasi-transitive graph

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/da48fef1-4c8b-46ff-a6cd-069240e48af5) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Critical-bond-and-site-percolation-on-the-cubic-lattice-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that nearest-neighbor Bernoulli bond and site percolation on ℤ<sup>3</sup> have no infinite cluster at their respective critical parameters. The proof combines a finite connection inequality for independent hyperedges with a finite-scale extension estimate and an adaptive exploration.
