# Optimal-order mixing of the Thorp shuffle

Result 238 · Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d29c1238-b169-49f4-8429-f41a1613c9ef) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Optimal-order-mixing-of-the-Thorp-shuffle-September-26-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that the Thorp shuffle on $`2^d`$ cards mixes in $`\Theta(d)`$ complete shuffles. The full permutation law after $`1600d`$ shuffles converges to uniform in total variation as $`d\to\infty`$, uniformly over the initial deck, while a support count gives a lower bound of $`2d-O(1)`$.
