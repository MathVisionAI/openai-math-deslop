# A surface counterexample to Shafarevich holomorphic convexity

Result 046 · Shafarevich counterexamples in dimension two and with large fundamental group

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/36432795-e1ea-4836-996a-b91d3a96f22f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-surface-counterexample-to-Shafarevich-holomorphic-convexity-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We construct a smooth connected projective complex surface whose universal cover is not holomorphically convex. This disproves the Shafarevich conjecture on holomorphic convexity in complex dimension two.
