# Absolutely Continuous Spectrum for Weak-Disorder Anderson Models in Dimensions at Least Three

Result 261 · Localization and delocalization in the Anderson model

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/853d8290-23ec-4f5f-96d3-316157d89397) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Absolutely-Continuous-Spectrum-for-Weak-Disorder-Anderson-Models-in-Dimensions-at-Least-Three-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

For each fixed dimension <i>d</i> ≥ 3 and each fixed sufficiently small positive disorder strength, we prove that the Anderson operator on ℤ<sup><i>d</i></sup> with independent uniform site potentials almost surely has purely absolutely continuous spectrum with nonzero weight on an open energy interval. The interval may depend on <i>d</i> but is independent of the disorder strength. This settles the purely absolutely continuous energy-range question in Simon's Problem 1 for this model.
