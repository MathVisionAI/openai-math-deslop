# The ordinary-double-point gap in every dimension

Result 037 · The ordinary-double-point volume gap

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/335a1c6f-9b99-4c15-928b-3c97e7173837) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-ordinary-double-point-gap-in-every-dimension-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the ordinary-double-point gap conjecture for boundary-zero complex algebraic klt germs in every dimension: a singular <i>n</i>-dimensional germ has normalized volume at most $`2(n-1)^n`$, with equality precisely at an analytic ordinary double point.
