# Endpoint pointwise convergence for the Schrodinger equation in higher dimensions

Result 080 · The exact Sobolev endpoint for Schrödinger convergence

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/23fd035d-471c-4699-8b21-a276dce0bc08) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Endpoint-pointwise-convergence-for-the-Schrodinger-equation-in-higher-dimensions-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We resolve the Sobolev endpoint of Carleson's pointwise convergence problem in every dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 3. For initial data in $`H^{n/(2(n+1))}(\mathbb R^n)`$, the free Schrödinger evolution converges almost everywhere to the initial data as $`t\downarrow0`$. The evolution is defined by removing Gaussian regularization on one full-measure spatial set, uniformly over the interval $`0\lt t\lt 1`$.
