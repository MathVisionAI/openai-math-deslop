# The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane $`\Re s\gt 7/8`$ 

Result 003 · The quasi-Riemann hypothesis

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/32827559-236b-4504-8ebf-d2b7a19ae32f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that all finite-order Hecke <i>L</i>-functions over $`\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})`$ and all Dirichlet <i>L</i>-functions are zero-free in the half-plane $`\Re s\gt 7/8`$, with the principal pole at <i>s</i> = 1 allowed. In particular, the Riemann zeta function is zero-free in this half-plane, proving the quasi-Riemann hypothesis.
