# A positive resolution of De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

Result 375 · De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/f61282b6-1e01-4f3b-a625-94a042c3c879) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/De-Giorgis-conjecture-in-dimension-eight-September-26-2026/article.pdf)

We resolve De Giorgi's conjecture positively in dimension eight: every entire <i>C</i><sup>2</sup> solution $`u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)`$ of $`\Delta u=u^3-u`$ with an everywhere positive directional derivative is a planar heteroclinic. We prove that every stable solution $`v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]`$ of this equation is a constant well or a planar heteroclinic, without an energy-growth assumption.
