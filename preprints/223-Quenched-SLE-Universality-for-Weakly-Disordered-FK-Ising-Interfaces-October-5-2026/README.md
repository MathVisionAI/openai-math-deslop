# Quenched SLE Universality for Weakly Disordered FK–Ising Interfaces

Result 223 · Random-cluster interfaces: critical, disordered, thermal, and natural-time scaling

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/392f3c51-ce0c-46bb-bc3f-f55cdb020b10) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Quenched-SLE-Universality-for-Weakly-Disordered-FK-Ising-Interfaces-October-5-2026/quenched-fk-ising.pdf)

We prove that critical FK–Ising interfaces with sufficiently weak, symmetric, independent two-valued bond disorder converge to chordal SLE<sub>16/3</sub>. The disorder strength is fixed as the mesh tends to zero, and convergence of the conditional curve laws holds in probability over the environment.
