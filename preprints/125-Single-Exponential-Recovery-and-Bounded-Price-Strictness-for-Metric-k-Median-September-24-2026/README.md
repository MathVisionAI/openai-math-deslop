# Single-exponential recovery and bounded-price strictness for metric k-median

Result 125 · The metric <i>k</i>-median approximation threshold and recovery

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/5c2c79af-eacd-4231-a51a-7bff77005d16) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Single-Exponential-Recovery-and-Bounded-Price-Strictness-for-Metric-k-Median-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We give an exact-budget recovery algorithm for metric <i>k</i>-median with single-exponential dependence on the number of comparison clusters without accurate, distinct proxies in a supplied anchor solution. On positive integral metrics of polynomially bounded diameter, a sufficiently small total proxy error and logarithmically many such clusters yield a $`(1+2/e+\varepsilon)`$ approximation in polynomial time with arbitrarily high success probability. We also prove bounded-price strictness for one compatible execution of the logarithmic-surplus construction. Together the recovery and payment arguments give a randomized $`(2-\sigma)`$ approximation, for an absolute <i>σ</i> &gt; 0, on arbitrary finite rational metrics, both with high probability and in expectation, while opening at most <i>k</i> facilities on every output.
