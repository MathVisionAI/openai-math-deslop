# A counterexample to integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

Result 361 · Failure of integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/4b0dc1b1-88e7-4a6c-8a1c-9aaff7e64d19) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-counterexample-to-integer-degree-harmonic-dimension-comparison-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

For some even <i>n</i> ≥ 8 and integer <i>k</i> ≥ 2, we construct a complete smooth metric on ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup> with nonnegative Ricci curvature whose space of real harmonic functions of pointwise polynomial growth at most <i>k</i> has dimension larger than the Euclidean harmonic-polynomial dimension. The metric is Euclidean near the origin, has asymptotic volume ratio strictly between zero and one, and has nonunique tangent cones at infinity. This answers the integer-degree form of Yau's dimension comparison question in the negative.
