# The E×CM component of Zilber–Pink for curves in A2

Result 016 · Zilber–Pink in abelian varieties and the Siegel threefold

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d49729b9-65f0-41b9-8d39-c989ffbc4a38) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-E-times-CM-Component-of-Zilber-Pink-for-Curves-in-A2-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the $`E\times\mathrm{CM}`$ component of Zilber–Pink for Hodge-generic algebraic curves in $`\mathcal A_2`$ over $`\overline{\mathbb Q}`$. Each such curve contains only finitely many points whose abelian surface is isogenous to a product of elliptic curves with at least one factor having complex multiplication.
