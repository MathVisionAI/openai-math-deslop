# Relative independence of the separable quotient problem

Result 323 · Independence of the separable quotient problem

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/ea6d708e-1a99-4d66-a470-62d4266e800d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Relative-independence-of-the-separable-quotient-problem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

The separable quotient problem asks whether every infinite-dimensional Banach space has a separable infinite-dimensional quotient. We prove that this statement is independent of ZFC, relative to the consistency of ZFC with a measurable cardinal. This holds over both the real and complex fields. For spaces of norm density ℵ<sub>1</sub>, we obtain independence relative to the consistency of ZFC alone.
