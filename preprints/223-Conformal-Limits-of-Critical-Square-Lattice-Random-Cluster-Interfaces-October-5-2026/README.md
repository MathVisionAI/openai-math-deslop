# Conformal Limits of Critical Square-Lattice Random-Cluster Interfaces

Result 223 · Random-cluster interfaces: critical, disordered, thermal, and natural-time scaling

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/c3a9ab79-a78e-46d8-9527-d80b1b0a8463) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Conformal-Limits-of-Critical-Square-Lattice-Random-Cluster-Interfaces-October-5-2026/paper.pdf)

For every fixed $`1\le q\le4`$, critical square-lattice random-cluster Dobrushin interfaces converge to chordal $`\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$, where $`\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$, and the complete nested plane loop collections converge to whole-plane $`\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$. The results hold under uniform marked Jordan boundary approximation and retain loop multiplicities and traversals. At <i>q</i> = 1 we obtain Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.
