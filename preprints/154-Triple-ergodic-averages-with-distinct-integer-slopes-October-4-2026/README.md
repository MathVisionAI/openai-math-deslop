# Triple ergodic averages with distinct integer slopes

Result 154 · Pointwise multiple ergodic averages for mixing transformations

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/cd488a15-c159-4ebc-a33f-20f95f4e5ad6) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Triple-ergodic-averages-with-distinct-integer-slopes-October-4-2026/triple-ergodic-distinct-slopes.pdf)

For every invertible mixing probability-preserving transformation, triple ergodic averages of bounded measurable functions along any three pairwise distinct nonzero integer slopes converge almost everywhere to the product of their integrals. The slopes may be positive or negative.
