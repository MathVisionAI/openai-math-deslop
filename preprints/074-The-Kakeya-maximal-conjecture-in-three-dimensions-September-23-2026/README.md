# The Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions

Result 074 · Kakeya in three and four dimensions

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/65daed8b-2d80-4bef-9601-d2e78620e618) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Kakeya-maximal-conjecture-in-three-dimensions-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions. For every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0, the maximal average over unit tubes of radius <i>δ</i> maps $`L^3(\mathbb R^3)`$ to $`L^3(S^2)`$ with norm at most $`C_\varepsilon\delta^{-\varepsilon}`$.
