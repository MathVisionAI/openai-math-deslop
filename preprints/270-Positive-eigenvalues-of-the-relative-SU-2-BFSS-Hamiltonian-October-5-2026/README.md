# Positive eigenvalues of the relative SU(2) BFSS Hamiltonian

Result 270 · Threshold and positive-energy bound states of the BFSS matrix model

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/7523778c-373c-425e-99f0-0ba1bca24706) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Positive-eigenvalues-of-the-relative-SU-2-BFSS-Hamiltonian-October-5-2026/positive-eigenvalues-relative-su2-bfss.pdf)

We prove that the relative $`\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (2)`$ BFSS Hamiltonian has infinitely many positive eigenvalues tending to infinity, with square-integrable eigenvectors. The operator is defined by closing the gauge-invariant supercharge form. This refutes, at <i>N</i> = 2, the exclusion of normalizable positive-energy states stated in the original BFSS paper.
