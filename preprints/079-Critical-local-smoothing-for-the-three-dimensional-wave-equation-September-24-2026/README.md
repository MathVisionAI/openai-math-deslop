# Critical local smoothing for the three-dimensional wave equation

Result 079 · Local smoothing in three dimensions

We prove the critical <i>L</i><sup>3</sup> local smoothing estimate for the wave equation in three spatial dimensions, with every positive Sobolev loss. This resolves Sogge's Euclidean local smoothing conjecture in dimension three.
