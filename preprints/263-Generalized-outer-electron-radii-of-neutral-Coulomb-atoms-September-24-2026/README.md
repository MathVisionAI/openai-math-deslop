# Generalized outer-electron radii of neutral Coulomb atoms

Result 263 · The ionization and generalized ionization conjectures

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/9c1a00dc-a23e-4a86-9499-e72a6a572386) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Generalized-outer-electron-radii-of-neutral-Coulomb-atoms-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the radius part of the generalized ionization conjecture for neutral nonrelativistic Coulomb atoms with two spin states. For every choice of ground states, the upper and lower large-nuclear-charge limits of the radius defined by an expected exterior electron mass <i>m</i> are both asymptotic to $`(81\pi^2/2)^{1/3}m^{-1/3}`$ as <i>m</i> tends to infinity.
