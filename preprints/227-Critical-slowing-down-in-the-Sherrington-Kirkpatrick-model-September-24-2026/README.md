# Critical slowing down in the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model

Result 227 · Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8ffd69f9-6246-42fd-b145-79d87e916f99) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Critical-slowing-down-in-the-Sherrington-Kirkpatrick-model-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

At the critical inverse temperature <i>β</i> = 1, we prove slow mixing for zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick heat-bath dynamics from typical equilibrium configurations held fixed as initial states. For every deterministic sequence $`t_n=o(n^{2/3})`$ in rate-one-per-site time, the Gibbs mass of initial states whose time-<i>t</i><sub><i>n</i></sub> total-variation distance from equilibrium exceeds 1/4 tends to one in probability over the disorder. The same statement holds for every deterministic integer sequence $`k_n=o(n^{5/3})`$ of uniform-site update attempts.
