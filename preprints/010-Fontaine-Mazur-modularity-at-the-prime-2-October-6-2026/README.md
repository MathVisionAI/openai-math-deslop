# Fontaine–Mazur modularity at the prime 2

Result 010 · Unrestricted pro-modularity at the prime two

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/63df7245-c9b1-4a26-94bd-4d4310a5b0f8) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Fontaine-Mazur-modularity-at-the-prime-2-October-6-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that every continuous, irreducible, odd two-dimensional 2-adic representation of $`G_{\mathbb Q}`$, unramified outside finitely many primes and de Rham at 2 with distinct Hodge–Tate weights, is modular up to Tate twist. This resolves the odd, regular two-dimensional Fontaine–Mazur conjecture over ℚ at 2, including all residual representations.
