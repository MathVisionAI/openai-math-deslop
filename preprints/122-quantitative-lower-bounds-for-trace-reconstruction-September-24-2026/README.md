# Quantitative lower bounds for trace reconstruction

Result 122 · Quantitative trace-reconstruction bounds with a uniform decoder

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/c8858c3c-40f2-4c19-879e-8c38c15222e2) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/quantitative-lower-bounds-for-trace-reconstruction-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

Exact worst-case reconstruction of a binary word from independent deletion traces requires $`n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}`$ samples for every fixed deletion probability $`q\in(0,1)`$, even with unrestricted computation and any fixed positive success probability. This gives a negative answer to the polynomial-sample question for binary trace reconstruction. More generally, when $`q^3\log n\to\infty`$, we prove a lower bound of $`n^{c\log(q^3\log n)}`$ samples for every fixed $`0\lt c\lt 1/(4\log2)`$. Here <i>q</i> is the known deletion probability, and all logarithms are natural.
