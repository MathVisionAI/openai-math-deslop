# Symplectic Balls in Symmetric Polar Products

Result 087 · The Mahler conjectures, functional inequalities and polar-product symplectic width

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/2655fa88-9669-4958-9d5d-471fa404a208) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Symplectic-Balls-in-Symmetric-Polar-Products-September-22-2026/paper.pdf)

For every integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2 and every origin-symmetric convex body $`K\subset\mathbb R^n`$, we prove that the Gromov width of $`\mathop{\mathrm{int}}\nolimits K\times\mathop{\mathrm{int}}\nolimits K^\circ`$ is 4. We construct smooth symplectic embeddings of standard balls of every capacity $`0\lt c\lt 4`$ into this polar product. Volume preservation then resolves the symmetric Mahler conjecture positively in every dimension.
