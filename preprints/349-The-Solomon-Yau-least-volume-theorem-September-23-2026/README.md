# The Solomon–Yau least-volume theorem

Result 349 · The Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/04058396-9f80-4414-9904-9259f5c062fc) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Solomon-Yau-least-volume-theorem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture for minimal hypersurfaces of unit round spheres: in every dimension <i>m</i> ≥ 2, a closed connected minimal immersion with non-totally-geodesic image has volume at least the smallest <i>m</i>-dimensional minimal Clifford product. Volume is measured on the domain, so covering multiplicities are included.
