# Nagata's conjecture for plane curves

Result 039 · Nagata’s conjecture and maximal Seshadri constants

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/1ab86c57-5851-42e8-8e3c-7789b2752a3d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Nagatas-Conjecture-for-Plane-Curves-September-23-2026/main.pdf)

We prove that a nonzero effective plane curve of degree <i>d</i> at <i>r</i> ≥ 10 very general complex points has total multiplicity strictly less than $`d\sqrt r`$. The inequality holds simultaneously for all curves, including reducible and nonreduced curves, and establishes Nagata's conjecture in its strict, nonhomogeneous form.
