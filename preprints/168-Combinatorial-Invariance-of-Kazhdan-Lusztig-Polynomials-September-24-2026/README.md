# Combinatorial invariance of Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials

Result 168 · Combinatorial invariance of Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/66f1aac3-d148-4152-a93f-ac467ace143e) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Combinatorial-Invariance-of-Kazhdan-Lusztig-Polynomials-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that an isomorphism of Bruhat intervals in arbitrary Coxeter systems preserves their equal-parameter Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials. This resolves the full combinatorial invariance conjecture positively.
