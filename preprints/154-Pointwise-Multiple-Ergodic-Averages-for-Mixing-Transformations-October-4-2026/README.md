# Pointwise Multiple Ergodic Averages for Mixing Transformations

Result 154 · Pointwise multiple ergodic averages for mixing transformations

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/a48ce706-693c-4e6b-947f-e4177ed27f5f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Pointwise-Multiple-Ergodic-Averages-for-Mixing-Transformations-October-4-2026/multiple-ergodic-averages.pdf)

Let <i>T</i> be an invertible mixing probability-preserving transformation. For every integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2 and every fixed tuple of bounded measurable functions, we prove that the consecutive multiple ergodic averages of length <i>n</i> converge almost everywhere to the product of the integrals, as the averaging length tends to infinity through all positive integers. The probability space need not be standard, and no rate of mixing is required.
