# Finite generation for the K(n)-local sphere

Result 313 · Finite generation for the $`K(n)`$-local sphere

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/8363c7e0-2366-4b8d-b8f8-84a38f9b235d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Finite-generation-for-the-Kn-local-sphere-September-24-2026/Finite-generation-for-the-Kn-local-sphere-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove that the homotopy groups of the $`K(n)`$-local sphere are finitely generated ℤ<sub><i>p</i></sub>-modules in every integer degree, for every prime <i>p</i> and positive height <i>n</i>. Equivalently, the same conclusion holds after $`K(n)`$-localizing any finite <i>p</i>-local spectrum. This answers the degreewise finiteness question of Hovey and Hovey–Strickland.
