# Numerical Semiampleness of Nef Adjoint Divisors

Result 036 · Numerical semiampleness and generalized minimal models

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/63c8675f-3452-42b8-8073-0805e6cf4a4f) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Numerical-Semiampleness-of-Nef-Adjoint-Divisors-October-3-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the Generalised Abundance Conjecture: if $`(X,B)`$ is a projective klt ℚ-pair over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, $`K_X+B`$ is pseudo-effective, <i>M</i> is a nef ℚ-Cartier divisor on <i>X</i>, and $`K_X+B+M`$ is nef, then $`K_X+B+M`$ is numerically equivalent to a semiample ℚ-Cartier divisor on <i>X</i>.
