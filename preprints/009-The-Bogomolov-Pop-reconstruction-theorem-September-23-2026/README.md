# The Bogomolov-Pop reconstruction theorem

Result 009 · Function-field reconstruction from Milnor K-theory and Galois data

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/4a8cac29-8673-4594-b096-e32015ac86ca) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Bogomolov-Pop-reconstruction-theorem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the Bogomolov–Pop reconstruction conjecture for function fields of transcendence degree at least two over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from <i>ℓ</i>. The pro-<i>ℓ</i> abelian-by-central datum determines the perfect closure and its constant field, with precisely the Frobenius and <i>ℓ</i>-adic unit ambiguities.
