# Marked polygon correlations and one-arc bounds

Result 237 · The three-quarter exponent for honeycomb self-avoiding walk

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/cc80bdba-5b9d-496d-9be5-45d9851bce42) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Marked-polygon-correlations-and-one-arc-bounds-September-26-2026/main.pdf)

At the critical honeycomb vertex activity, the squared-length mass of simple polygons of diameter at most <i>H</i>, counted modulo translations, is at most $`H^{2/3+o(1)}`$. We also prove a quantitative two-mark cylinder estimate and a polynomial one-arc bound uniform even for arbitrarily unequal marked intervals.
