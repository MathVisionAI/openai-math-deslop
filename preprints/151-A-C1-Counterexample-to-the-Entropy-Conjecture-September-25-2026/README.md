# A C^1 Counterexample to the Entropy Conjecture

Result 151 · A <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> counterexample to the entropy conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/34c66b71-2872-4b02-955a-2f4d1c05af68) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-C1-Counterexample-to-the-Entropy-Conjecture-September-25-2026/article.pdf)

We disprove the general <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> self-map formulation of Shub's entropy conjecture. We construct a noninvertible <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> self-map of a compact smooth manifold without boundary whose topological entropy is zero, while its action on second real homology has eigenvalue 2. Thus the topological entropy is strictly smaller than the logarithm of the homological spectral radius.
