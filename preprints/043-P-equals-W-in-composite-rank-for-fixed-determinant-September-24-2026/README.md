# P=W in composite rank for fixed determinant

Result 043 · <i>P</i> = <i>W</i> for fixed-determinant SL<sub><i>n</i></sub> moduli spaces

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/5269f452-e256-407c-bd0a-1dfc689517c2) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/P-equals-W-in-composite-rank-for-fixed-determinant-September-24-2026/P-equals-W-in-composite-rank-for-fixed-determinant-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the <i>P</i> = <i>W</i> conjecture on the full rational cohomology of fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces in composite rank and coprime degree, for smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two. Together with the established prime-rank cases, this gives the equality in every coprime rank.
