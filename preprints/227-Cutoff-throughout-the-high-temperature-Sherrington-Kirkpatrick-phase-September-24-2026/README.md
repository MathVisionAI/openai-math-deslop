# Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington–Kirkpatrick phase

Result 227 · Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/15e31fc0-0ef4-4fec-9446-556cdc858d95) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Cutoff-throughout-the-high-temperature-Sherrington-Kirkpatrick-phase-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove worst-case total-variation cutoff for the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick heat-bath dynamics at every fixed inverse temperature $`0\leq\beta\lt 1`$. With rate-one refresh at each spin, the cutoff location is $`\log n/(2\lambda(\beta))`$ for a positive deterministic rate $`\lambda(\beta)`$. The location for uniformly chosen single-site update attempts is <i>n</i> times as large. Convergence is in probability over the disorder.
