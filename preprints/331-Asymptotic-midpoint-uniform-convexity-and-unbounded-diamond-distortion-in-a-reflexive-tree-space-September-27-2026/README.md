# Asymptotic midpoint uniform convexity and unbounded diamond distortion in a reflexive tree space

Result 331 · Reflexive midpoint convexity and diamond distortion

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/2e5b5ce5-e0aa-47e7-b6d0-81e8dd80fdff) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Asymptotic-midpoint-uniform-convexity-and-unbounded-diamond-distortion-in-a-reflexive-tree-space-September-27-2026/manuscript.pdf)

We construct a separable reflexive real Banach space whose given norm is asymptotically midpoint uniformly convex but which admits no asymptotically uniformly convex equivalent norm. Its averaged midpoint modulus is at least $`\sqrt{1+t^2/12}-1`$, and the countably branching diamond of depth <i>k</i> has distortion at least $`\sqrt{1+k/12}`$ in this space. This gives a negative answer to the reflexive diamond converse for asymptotic uniform convexifiability.
