# Arithmetic Stein-degree bounds for log Calabi–Yau pairs

Result 034 · Log abundance for compact Kähler spaces under logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/170dc7e3-f526-4416-8da6-90b59155fc03) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Arithmetic-Stein-degree-bounds-for-log-Calabi-Yau-pairs-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

Fix <i>d</i> ≥ 1 and <i>t</i> &gt; 0. Let $`(X,B)`$ be an ordinary projective log canonical ℚ-pair of dimension <i>d</i> over a characteristic-zero field <i>k</i>, with <i>X</i> normal and integral, $`H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=k`$, <i>B</i> effective, and $`K_X+B\sim_{\mathbb Q}0`$. We prove that every prime component <i>S</i> of <i>B</i> with coefficient at least <i>t</i> satisfies $`[k_S:k]\leq N(d,t)`$, where <i>k</i><sub><i>S</i></sub> is the relative algebraic closure of <i>k</i> in $`k(S)`$. This also bounds the Stein degree of <i>S</i> over <i>k</i>, proving the contraction-to-a-point formulation of Birkar's Stein-degree conjecture for ordinary ℚ-pairs.
