# Ample rank-two bundles on the quadric surface without Griffiths-positive metrics

Result 050 · A counterexample to Griffiths’ positivity conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/bd924ba9-ac30-4136-8b9b-342571af80ac) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/ample-rank-two-bundles-on-the-quadric-surface-without-griffiths-positive-metrics-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We give counterexamples to Griffiths' conjecture in rank two on the quadric surface $`\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1`$. We construct an explicit bundle <i>G</i> whose coordinatewise power pullbacks, tensored with $`\mathcal O(1,1)`$, are ample for every positive power, but admit no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric for all sufficiently large powers.
