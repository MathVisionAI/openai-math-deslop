# A Capacity Criterion for Four-State Potts Reconstruction on Trees

Result 229 · Exact three- and four-state reconstruction thresholds and four-state tree capacity

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/63b892a4-654b-4601-8c7a-77b1954905e5) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/A-Capacity-Criterion-for-Four-State-Potts-Reconstruction-on-Trees-October-5-2026/four-state-capacity.pdf)

For the ferromagnetic four-state broadcast model with $`0\lt \lambda\lt 1`$, we prove that reconstruction on a bounded-degree deterministic rooted tree occurs exactly when its <i>L</i><sup>3</sup> capacity with edge resistances $`\lambda^{-2|e|}`$ is positive. This gives an exact criterion without regularity or growth-rate assumptions on the tree, including at the exponential critical boundary.
