# Quaternionic division points on curves in the Siegel threefold

Result 016 · Zilber–Pink in abelian varieties and the Siegel threefold

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/6d391ae1-2fa1-43ca-ae37-7e7ee887761d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Quaternionic-Division-Points-on-Curves-in-the-Siegel-Threefold-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the quaternionic-division component of Zilber–Pink for curves in $`\mathcal A_2`$ over $`\overline{\mathbb Q}`$. A Hodge-generic algebraic curve contains only finitely many points whose full geometric rational endomorphism algebra is an indefinite quaternion division algebra over ℚ. No boundary or reduction hypothesis is required.
