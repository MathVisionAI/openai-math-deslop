# Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

Result 150 · Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/d2e78105-6da4-4c51-bdff-064737167d3a) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Weak-mixing-of-triangular-billiards-with-an-irrational-angle-October-5-2026/weak-mixing-triangular-billiards.pdf)

We prove that the unit-speed billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to <i>π</i> is weakly mixing for normalized Liouville measure. Equivalently, the product of the flow with itself is ergodic.
