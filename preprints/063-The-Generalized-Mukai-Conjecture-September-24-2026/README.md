# The generalized Mukai conjecture

Result 063 · The generalized Mukai conjecture

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/6128d9a9-51cd-4e76-8bc5-8daee5cd3744) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Generalized-Mukai-Conjecture-September-24-2026/article.pdf)

We prove the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex Fano manifold of dimension <i>n</i>, Picard number <i>ρ</i>, and pseudoindex <i>ι</i> satisfies $`\rho(\iota-1)\le n`$. Equality holds precisely for the product of <i>ρ</i> copies of $`\mathbb P^{\iota-1}`$.
