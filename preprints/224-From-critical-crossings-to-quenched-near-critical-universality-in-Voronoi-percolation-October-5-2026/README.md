# From critical crossings to quenched near-critical universality in Voronoi percolation

Result 224 · Critical and quenched near-critical universality for Poisson–Voronoi percolation

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/45998730-c2af-4af4-9ee7-741d9eb2ff30) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/From-critical-crossings-to-quenched-near-critical-universality-in-Voronoi-percolation-October-5-2026/critical-crossings-quenched-near-critical-universality-voronoi-percolation.pdf)

Taking Cardy's crossing formula for critical Poisson–Voronoi percolation as an input, we prove a universal joint limit for the near-critical crossing thresholds of rational polygonal quadrilaterals under the monotone coupling. Conditional on the Poisson tessellation, the threshold law converges in probability over tessellations to the same law as on the triangular lattice. Each model is normalized by its own expected number of color-pivotal sites for a unit-square crossing.
