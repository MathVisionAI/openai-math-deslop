# Functional universality of critical SK autocorrelations

Result 227 · Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/faa24449-a56f-4f6c-aa48-66a3cd966ef5) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Functional-universality-of-critical-SK-autocorrelations-October-5-2026/critical-sk-autocorrelations.pdf)

We prove that the stationary spin autocorrelation of the zero-field Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model at inverse temperature <i>β</i> = 1 has a common functional scaling limit for Gaussian and Rademacher couplings. With rate-one heat-bath clocks at every site, time is scaled by <i>n</i><sup>2/3</sup> and the mean spin autocorrelation is multiplied by <i>n</i><sup>1/3</sup>. The functions converge in law uniformly on compact positive-time intervals. Their limiting law is not a point mass: it retains sample-to-sample randomness, and its functions decay to zero at large times.
