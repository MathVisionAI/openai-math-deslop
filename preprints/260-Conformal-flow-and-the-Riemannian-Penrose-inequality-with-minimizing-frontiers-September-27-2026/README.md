# Conformal flow and the Riemannian Penrose inequality with minimizing frontiers

Result 260 · Spacetime Penrose inequalities: enclosing area, charge, rotation, and anti-de Sitter extensions

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/7cd38f0d-ee26-4231-b0ad-48397c07a99d) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/Conformal-flow-and-the-Riemannian-Penrose-inequality-with-minimizing-frontiers-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)

We give a detailed conformal-flow proof of the numerical Riemannian Penrose inequality in every dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 3 for complete asymptotically flat exteriors with nonnegative scalar curvature and full compact frontiers that are outer minimizing and locally perimeter minimizing. The metric extends smoothly through the possibly singular frontier; neither spin nor frontier connectedness is assumed. The proof follows the conformal-flow approach of Bray, Bray–Lee, and Bi–Zhu.
