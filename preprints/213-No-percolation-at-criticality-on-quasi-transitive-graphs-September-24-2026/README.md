# No percolation at criticality on quasi-transitive graphs

Result 213 · Critical percolation on every quasi-transitive graph

[Read in MathVision Reading Mode](https://app.mathvision.ai/share/pdfs/7c9167c1-9838-4271-9766-b48da7f41559) · [Original PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/No-percolation-at-criticality-on-quasi-transitive-graphs-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that critical Bernoulli bond percolation has no infinite cluster on any infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability less than one. This resolves the bond form of the criticality conjecture of Benjamini and Schramm.
